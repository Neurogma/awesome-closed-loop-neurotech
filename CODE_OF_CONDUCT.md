# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role in the project.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and other participants to act professionally, constructively, and in good faith.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, documentation, or evidence rather than individuals.
- Welcoming different technical perspectives and levels of experience.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Clearly distinguishing facts, evidence, assumptions, and opinions.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them when identified.
- Respecting project scope, contribution guidelines, and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private or sensitive information about another person without permission.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats of violence or other threatening behavior.
- Deliberate attempts to damage, compromise, or abuse project infrastructure.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Repository discussions and comments.
- Project-related communication and collaboration.
- Other spaces where an individual is representing or participating in this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- Requesting that a participant stop specific behavior.
- Removing or editing inappropriate content.
- Closing issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Restricting participation in project spaces.
- Temporarily or permanently banning participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the severity and context of the behavior.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately using the contact method published in the repository's maintainer information.

Please include enough information to allow the maintainers to understand what happened and, where appropriate, links or other relevant evidence.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
